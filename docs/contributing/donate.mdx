Thank you very much for considering supporting the Floorp Projects!

## Why We Need Your Support

The development and operation of Floorp are supported by technical contributions, ad revenue, donations, and more from our community. In addition to an open development structure and funding to sustain project infrastructure, we believe a viable revenue foundation that sustains the project as a business is also essential.

For example, expanding features may require not only technical implementation, but also negotiations and contracts with external partners, as well as the operational structure needed to support them. Your support helps sustain daily development and operations, while building the foundation for the future.

For more details, please check [this blog post](https://blog.floorp.app/notice/260913-operational-transparency).

## Contribute to the Project

There are many ways to support the project beyond financial contributions, such as providing code, improving localization, and helping other users.

Please check [this page](/docs/contributing/floorp) for more details.

## View Ads on Our Websites

Advertisements may be displayed on our [official website](https://floorp.app) and the [Floorp Blog](https://blog.floorp.app). Revenue is used to fund the operation of the Floorp Projects.

Whether to display ads on the release notes shown after updating Floorp can be managed in Floorp Hub > Updates & Experiments (`about:hub#/about/updates`) or from extension settings.

## Donate to the Project

We accept donations via [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/Ryosuke-Asano) or [Buy Me a Coffee](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/surapunoyousei).

To all our sponsors: thank you so much for your support!

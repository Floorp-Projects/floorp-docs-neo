Floorp is an open-source web browser driven by the community. Your support is essential for ensuring the project's sustainability and covering costs like server maintenance.

Thank you for considering supporting the Floorp Projects!

## Contribute to the Project

There are many ways to support the project beyond financial contributions, such as providing code, improving localization, and helping other users.

Please check [this page](/docs/contributing/floorp) for more details.

## View Ads on Our Websites

The advertisements displayed on our [official website](https://floorp.app) and the [Floorp Blog](https://blog.floorp.app) help cover the maintenance costs of these sites.

## Donate to the Project

We accept donations via [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/Ryosuke-Asano) or [Buy Me a Coffee](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/surapunoyousei).

To all our sponsors: thank you so much for your support!

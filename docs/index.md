### Welcome to the Floorp User Documentation!

In this documentation, you can learn how to use Floorp Browser features, explore the Floorp project, and find useful resources.

### How to Troubleshoot

1. **Check the "Issue Tracker" section**: Explains common problems and their solutions.
2. **Report issues**: If you still can't find a solution, please review the [checklist](/docs/other/issue-checklist) and report the issue on our [GitHub page](https://github.com/Floorp-Projects/Floorp/issues/new/choose) or [Discord community](https://discord.floorp.app).

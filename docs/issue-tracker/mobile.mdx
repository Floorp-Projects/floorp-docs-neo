### iOS
Floorp for iOS is being built on top of [Firefox for iOS](https://github.com/mozilla-mobile/firefox-ios). Source: [Floorp-Projects/floorp-ios](https://github.com/Floorp-Projects/floorp-ios).

### Android
No Android version is planned at this time. Our resources are limited, and every additional platform multiplies maintenance costs.
